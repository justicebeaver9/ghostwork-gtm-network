# Ghostwork Labs GTM Network — Monetization & Launch Playbook

## Core Offer
A curated, high-signal source for US SaaS and technology sales / GTM roles that surfaces the information candidates actually need: true OTE ranges, sales team size and composition, quota attainability, ramp reality, equity, and company trajectory signals.

Tagline: **Talent moves in the dark.**

## Revenue Layers

### 1. Candidate Subscriptions (Primary, Predictable)
- **$29/month** or **$249/year** (save ~28%)
- Unlocks: full compensation details, team insights, quota/ramp data, company signals, weekly newsletter digest, job alerts
- Free tier: limited listings with blurred insights to drive conversion
- Target: 200–500 paying subscribers in year 1 = $70K–$175K ARR

### 2. Sponsored / Featured Roles (High Margin)
- Companies pay to have roles featured at the top, with full insights unlocked for all viewers, or highlighted in the newsletter
- Pricing ideas:
  - Single featured role: $750–1,500
  - Monthly featured package (2–3 roles): $2,000–4,000
  - Newsletter sponsorship: $500–1,500 per issue
- Low fulfillment cost; pure margin

### 3. Placement Fees / Recruiting Engine (Highest Upside)
Two models:
- **Direct representation**: Candidates opt in to be represented by Ghostwork Labs. You source, screen, and present. Standard contingency fee of 15–25% of first-year base or OTE (market rate for sales roles is typically 18–25%).
- **Tracked inbound**: Candidates apply through the platform. If hired, company pays a reduced success fee (10–18%) or you invoice based on attribution.
- On a $250K OTE AE, 20% = $50K per placement. Even 4–6 placements/year is material revenue.
- This turns the newsletter/job board into a top-of-funnel for the existing Ghostwork Labs recruiting practice.

### Additional optional layers
- Company talent intelligence reports (already in brand)
- Premium alerts / Slack channel for high-volume hiring companies
- Data licensing (anonymized compensation benchmarks)

## Payment Setup (Stripe)
1. Create a Stripe account linked to Ghostwork Labs / your entity.
2. Create two Products:
   - Monthly subscription: $29
   - Annual subscription: $249
3. Create Payment Links for each (Dashboard → Payment Links → New).
4. Optional: Featured role one-time payment links ($750, $1,500, etc.).
5. Paste the Payment Link URLs into the site subscribe buttons and newsletter CTAs.
6. Enable Stripe Customer Portal so subscribers can manage billing.

Beehiiv or Ghost is recommended for the newsletter (0% platform cut once on paid plan). Connect Stripe directly.

## Launch Sequence
1. Deploy this site to Vercel (connect the GitHub repo).
2. Set up Stripe Payment Links and update the site.
3. Create Beehiiv (or Ghost) publication: ghostworklabs.beehiiv.com or custom domain.
4. Seed with 20–40 real roles (scrape public ATS boards + outreach for exclusive insights).
5. Publish first newsletter edition and share on LinkedIn / X / relevant communities.
6. Outreach to 20–30 SaaS companies offering featured placement packages.
7. Soft-launch placement partnership terms to companies that post roles.
