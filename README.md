# Ghostwork Labs — GTM Roles Network

**Talent moves in the dark.**

Private, high-signal job board and newsletter for SaaS and technology sales & GTM roles in the United States.

## What candidates get
- Real compensation (base + OTE ranges, equity)
- Sales team composition and structure
- Quota design, attainment reality, ramp expectations
- Company signals (funding, leadership changes, motion, culture notes)
- Weekly curated digest

## Monetization layers
1. **Candidate subscriptions** — $29/month or $249/year for full access + alerts
2. **Featured roles** — Companies pay to highlight listings ($500–2,000 per role or monthly packages)
3. **Placement fees** — Ghostwork Labs represents candidates or tracks inbound and earns 10–25% of first-year OTE on successful hires

## Tech
Static site (Vercel / Netlify ready). Data currently inlined for MVP. Future: Airtable/Notion backend + auth + Stripe.
